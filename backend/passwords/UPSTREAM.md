# Offline password corpus

`common.json` contains the deduplicated, nonempty lines of SecLists' `Passwords/Common-Credentials/10k-most-common.txt` and `xato-net-10-million-passwords-10000.txt`, converted to JSON. Source revision is `9fe5162cb727286b513a03f33a5b61fb13f9deff`. License is in `LICENSE`.

Additional source: https://github.com/danielmiessler/SecLists/blob/9fe5162cb727286b513a03f33a5b61fb13f9deff/Passwords/Common-Credentials/xato-net-10-million-passwords-10000.txt

Refresh deliberately from a pinned revision, review the change, and rerun password screening tests. This is a finite common-password blocklist. It cannot detect every breached password. Registration never sends passwords or hashes to a third party.
