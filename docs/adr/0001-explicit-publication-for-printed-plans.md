# Publish explicit revisions for printed event plans

Hotel staff initially work from printed plans, so editing an online document alone cannot ensure kitchen, service, and marketing receive the same instructions. Marketing prepares drafts and deliberately publishes numbered, timestamped revisions with visible changes, rather than exposing each edit as current service instructions. Each new revision, including cancellation, requires a direct handoff and fresh receipt confirmations from the event's designated kitchen and service leads; marketing may record verbal confirmations with clear attribution so leads do not need to log in.

Leads with login accounts may confirm directly; the record distinguishes direct confirmation from verbal confirmation entered by marketing. Draft previews and prints are prominently labeled as unpublished and do not participate in receipt confirmation.
