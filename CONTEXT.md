# Venue Operations

This context describes the operational language shared by restaurant teams working across one or more physical venues.

## Language

**Live Operations**:
The current operational state of a venue, including what is ready, what needs attention, and who owns the response.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, activity feed

**Operational Area**:
One of the three perspectives through which venue work is coordinated: Management, Kitchen, or Service. An area groups work rather than permanently partitioning people.
_Avoid_: Department, team

**Management**:
The operational area covering announcements, assignments, shift oversight, and incidents.
_Avoid_: Organization, administration

**Kitchen**:
The operational area covering preparation, readiness, stock-outs, equipment issues, and handoffs.
_Avoid_: Back of house, BOH

**Service**:
The operational area covering opening and closing readiness, sections, guest issues, and handoffs.
_Avoid_: Front of house, FOH

**Organization**:
A restaurant operator responsible for one or more venues and the people who work in them.
_Avoid_: Account, restaurant group, tenant

**Team Member**:
A person with an individual identity who is granted access to an organization and may work in one or more of its venues.
_Avoid_: User, employee, worker

**Venue**:
A single physical restaurant location operated by an organization.
_Avoid_: Location, site, restaurant
